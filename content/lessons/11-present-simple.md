# Present Simple

Present simple adalah tense yang paling sering dipakai. Namanya "present", tetapi fungsinya bukan "sedang terjadi sekarang".

Present simple menyatakan hal yang **umum, rutin, atau permanen**.

> **Kunci:** Subject **he/she/it** (orang ketiga tunggal) → verb + **-s/-es**. Subject lain → verb dasar.

## A. Kapan dipakai

| Fungsi | Contoh |
| --- | --- |
| Kebiasaan / rutinitas | I **drink** coffee every morning. |
| Fakta umum & ilmiah | Water **boils** at 100°C. |
| Keadaan permanen | She **lives** in Surabaya. |
| Jadwal tetap (transport, acara) | The train **leaves** at 8 a.m. |
| Perasaan & pikiran (state verb) | I **know** the answer. |

## B. Bentuk

:::breakdown
She | S
works | V (+s)
at a bank | A
= Dia bekerja di bank.
:::

| | I / you / we / they | he / she / it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Positif | I **work**. | She **works**. |
| Negatif | I **don't work**. | She **doesn't work**. |
| Pertanyaan | **Do** you **work**? | **Does** she **work**? |

> **Ingat:** Di negatif dan pertanyaan, -s pindah ke **does**. Main verb kembali ke V1.

## C. Aturan ejaan -s / -es

| Akhiran verb | Aturan | Contoh |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Umumnya | + s | work → works, read → reads |
| -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o | + es | watch → watches, go → goes, fix → fixes |
| Konsonan + y | y → ies | study → studies, try → tries |
| Vokal + y | + s | play → plays, buy → buys |
| Tidak beraturan | — | have → **has**, be → **is** |

## D. Kata keterangan yang sering muncul

**always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never, every day/week, once a week, on Mondays**

| Posisi | Contoh |
| --- | --- |
| Sebelum main verb | She **usually** walks to work. |
| Setelah be | He **is** **always** on time. |
| Di akhir (frasa waktu) | We meet **every Friday**. |

## E. State verbs

Verb yang menyatakan **keadaan**, bukan tindakan, biasanya tetap present simple meskipun maknanya "sekarang".

| Kategori | Contoh |
| --- | --- |
| Pikiran | know, believe, understand, remember, mean |
| Perasaan | like, love, hate, want, need, prefer |
| Kepemilikan | have (milik), own, belong |
| Indra | see, hear, smell, taste |

❌ I am knowing the answer.
✅ I know the answer.

## F. Common Mistakes

### 1. Lupa -s untuk he/she/it
❌ My father work in a factory.
✅ My father works in a factory.

### 2. -s ganda di negatif/pertanyaan
❌ She doesn't likes tea.
✅ She doesn't like tea.

❌ Does he plays football?
✅ Does he play football?

### 3. Be + verb
❌ I am work at a hospital.
✅ I work at a hospital.

Bahasa Indonesia tidak punya "am" di "saya bekerja", jadi jangan tambahkan be sebelum verb biasa.

### 4. Has vs have
❌ She have two cats.
✅ She has two cats.

## Ringkasan

- Dipakai untuk **kebiasaan, fakta, keadaan permanen, jadwal**.
- **he/she/it** → verb + s/es; negatif/pertanyaan → **does** + V1.
- Jangan tambahkan **am/is/are** sebelum verb biasa.
- State verbs (know, like, want) tetap present simple.
