# Sentence Elements

Pada Lesson 1, kita sudah mempelajari **parts of speech**: noun, verb, adjective, adverb, dan lainnya.
Sekarang kita pelajari bagaimana kata-kata itu **bekerja sama membentuk kalimat**.
Materi ini dasar untuk tenses, passive voice, relative clauses, hingga complex sentences.

## A. Parts of Speech vs Sentence Elements

Keduanya konsep yang berbeda.

| Parts of Speech | Sentence Elements |
| --- | --- |
| Jenis atau kelas kata | Fungsi dalam struktur kalimat |
| Noun, Verb, Adjective, Adverb | Subject, Verb, Object, Complement, Adverbial |
| Menjawab: "Kata ini jenis apa?" | Menjawab: "Apa peran frasa ini di kalimat?" |

:::breakdown
The student | S
reads | V
a book | O
carefully | A
= Siswa itu membaca sebuah buku dengan hati-hati.
:::

**Student** adalah noun (jenis kata), tetapi frasa **the student** berfungsi sebagai subject (elemen kalimat).
Satu elemen bisa terdiri dari banyak kata.

## B. Lima elemen utama kalimat

| Simbol | Elemen | Fungsi |
| --- | --- | --- |
| S | Subject | Siapa/apa yang dibicarakan |
| V | Verb | Tindakan atau keadaan |
| O | Object | Penerima/sasaran tindakan |
| C | Complement | Melengkapi makna subject atau object |
| A | Adverbial | Info tambahan: waktu, tempat, cara, alasan |

### 1. Subject (S)
Pokok pembicaraan. Bentuknya noun, pronoun, noun phrase, bahkan verb-ing.

- **She** studies English.
- **The students in my class** are intelligent.
- **Learning English** takes time.

> **Tips:** Cari verb dulu, lalu tanya **"Siapa/apa yang ...?"**
> The engineer develops a new system. → Who develops? **The engineer** = Subject.

### 2. Verb (V)
Inti predikat. Bisa satu kata atau **verb phrase**.

:::breakdown
She | S
has been studying | V
English | O
:::

**Has been studying** dihitung sebagai satu elemen V.

**Transitive vs Intransitive**

| Jenis | Butuh object? | Contoh |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transitive | Ya | She **reads** a book. |
| Intransitive | Tidak | The baby **sleeps**. |
| Keduanya | Tergantung konteks | I **eat** every morning. / I **eat** breakfast. |

Jadi, jangan menghafal bahwa satu verb hanya punya satu pola.

### 3. Object (O)
Penerima tindakan dari transitive verb.

- **Direct Object (DO):** yang langsung dikenai tindakan. She bought **a laptop**. → What did she buy?
- **Indirect Object (IO):** penerima sesuatu.

:::breakdown
She | S
gave | V
me | IO
a gift | DO
:::

Bandingkan: She gave a gift **to me**. Di sini **a gift** tetap DO, sedangkan **to me** adalah prepositional phrase yang menunjukkan penerima.

### 4. Complement (C)
Menjelaskan atau mengidentifikasi subject/object. Sering tertukar dengan object.

**Subject Complement (SC)** muncul setelah linking verb: be, seem, become, look, feel, taste.

:::breakdown
She | S
is | V
a doctor | SC
:::

A doctor menjelaskan **identitas She**, bukan menerima tindakan.

**Object Complement (OC)** menjelaskan object.

:::breakdown
The news | S
made | V
her | O
sad | OC
:::

**Object vs Complement**

| Kalimat | Elemen terakhir | Alasan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| She bought a car. | Object | Mobil menerima tindakan |
| She is a teacher. | Subject Complement | Identitas subject |
| He looks tired. | Subject Complement | Kondisi subject |
| They made him angry. | Object Complement | Kondisi object |

### 5. Adverbial (A)
Keterangan waktu, tempat, cara, alasan, frekuensi.

| Kalimat | Adverbial | Bentuk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| He drives quickly. | quickly | Adverb |
| He studies at night. | at night | Prepositional phrase |
| They met last week. | last week | Noun phrase |
| He smiled when he saw her. | when he saw her | Clause |

> **Ingat:** **Adverb** = jenis kata. **Adverbial** = fungsi. Tidak semua adverbial adalah adverb.

Tidak semua adverbial opsional: She put the book **on the table** — tanpa frasa tempat, kalimat tidak lengkap.

## C. Tujuh pola dasar kalimat

:::patterns
S + V | The baby sleeps. | The baby = S; sleeps = V
S + V + C | She is intelligent. | intelligent = C
S + V + A | She lives in Malang. | in Malang = A (wajib)
S + V + O | He reads a book. | a book = O
S + V + O + O | She gave me a gift. | me = IO; a gift = DO
S + V + O + C | They made him angry. | angry = OC
S + V + O + A | She put the book on the table. | on the table = A (wajib)
:::

Kalimat nyata bisa lebih panjang, tetapi intinya tetap salah satu pola ini.

:::breakdown
My younger brother | S
bought | V
a new laptop | O
yesterday | A
= Pola inti S + V + O, ditambah adverbial waktu.
:::

## D. Common Mistakes

### 1. Menganggap semua noun adalah subject
❌ Book selalu subject karena noun.
✅ The book is interesting. → Subject
✅ She reads the book. → Object

### 2. Menganggap kata setelah verb selalu object
❌ She is happy. → happy = object
✅ Happy adalah subject complement karena menjelaskan subject.

### 3. Memaksakan object pada intransitive verb
❌ He sleeps a bed.
✅ He sleeps in a bed.

In a bed adalah adverbial of place, bukan object.

### 4. Menyamakan adverb dengan adverbial
❌ In the morning adalah adverb.
✅ In the morning adalah prepositional phrase yang berfungsi sebagai adverbial.

## Ringkasan

- Cari **V** → tanya siapa/apa pelakunya (**S**).
- Ada yang dikenai tindakan? → **O**.
- Ada yang menjelaskan S atau O? → **C**.
- Sisanya (waktu, tempat, cara, alasan) → **A**.
