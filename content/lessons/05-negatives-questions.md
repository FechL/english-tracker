# Negatives & Questions

Bahasa Indonesia cukup menambah "tidak" atau nada bertanya.

Bahasa Inggris membutuhkan **auxiliary** (kata kerja bantu) untuk membuat kalimat negatif dan pertanyaan.

> **Kunci:** Jika kalimat sudah punya auxiliary (be, have, can, will...), pakai itu. Jika tidak, tambahkan **do / does / did**.

## A. Dua jenis kalimat

| Kalimat punya auxiliary? | Negatif | Pertanyaan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ya (be, can, will, have...) | Tambah **not** setelahnya | **Pindahkan** auxiliary ke depan |
| Tidak (hanya main verb) | **do/does/did + not + V1** | **Do/Does/Did + S + V1?** |

## B. Kalimat dengan auxiliary

| Positif | Negatif | Pertanyaan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| She **is** busy. | She **is not** busy. | **Is** she busy? |
| They **can** swim. | They **cannot** swim. | **Can** they swim? |
| He **has** finished. | He **has not** finished. | **Has** he finished? |
| We **will** come. | We **will not** come. | **Will** we come? |

:::breakdown
Is | V
she | S
busy | SC
= Be (is) pindah ke depan subject.
:::

## C. Kalimat tanpa auxiliary: pakai do

| Subject & waktu | Auxiliary | Contoh |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I / you / we / they (present) | do | They **do not** work. / **Do** they work? |
| he / she / it (present) | does | She **does not** work. / **Does** she work? |
| Semua subject (past) | did | He **did not** work. / **Did** he work? |

:::breakdown
Does | AUX
she | S
work | V
here | A
:::

> **Ingat:** Setelah do/does/did, main verb kembali ke **bentuk dasar (V1)**. -s dan -ed "pindah" ke auxiliary.

❌ She doesn't works.
✅ She doesn't work.

❌ Did you went?
✅ Did you go?

## D. Bentuk singkat (contractions)

| Panjang | Singkat |
| --- | --- |
| is not / are not | isn't / aren't |
| do not / does not / did not | don't / doesn't / didn't |
| cannot / will not | can't / won't |
| have not / has not | haven't / hasn't |

I am not → **I'm not** (bukan "I amn't").

## E. Short answers

Jawab dengan **auxiliary yang sama** dengan pertanyaan.

- **Do** you like coffee? → Yes, I **do**. / No, I **don't**.
- **Is** she ready? → Yes, she **is**. / No, she **isn't**.
- **Can** you swim? → Yes, I **can**. / No, I **can't**.

❌ Do you like coffee? — Yes, I like.
✅ Yes, I do.

## F. Common Mistakes

### 1. Lupa auxiliary
❌ You like coffee?
✅ Do you like coffee?

(Tanpa do hanya bisa di percakapan santai dengan intonasi naik.)

### 2. Double tense
❌ She didn't came.
✅ She didn't come.

### 3. Do dengan be
❌ Do you are a student?
✅ Are you a student?

### 4. Not di tempat yang salah
❌ I not like it.
✅ I don't like it.

### 5. Double negative
❌ I don't have nothing.
✅ I don't have anything. / I have nothing.

## Ringkasan

- Cari auxiliary. Ada? → tambah **not** / pindahkan ke depan.
- Tidak ada? → **do/does/did** + V1.
- Main verb setelah do/does/did selalu **V1**.
- Short answer memakai auxiliary yang sama.
