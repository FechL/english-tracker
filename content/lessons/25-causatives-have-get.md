# Causatives: Have & Get

Causative dipakai saat kamu **menyuruh atau mengatur orang lain** melakukan sesuatu untukmu.

Bandingkan: "I cut my hair" berarti kamu memotong rambutmu **sendiri**. "I had my hair cut" berarti **tukang cukur** yang memotongnya.

> **Kunci:** **have/get + object + V3** = sesuatu dikerjakan oleh orang lain untukmu (seperti passive, tapi subject-nya yang mengatur).

## A. Pola utama: have/get something done

:::breakdown
I | S
had | V (have)
my car | O
repaired | OC (V3)
yesterday | A (time)
= Mobil saya diperbaiki (oleh bengkel) kemarin.
:::

| Tense | Contoh |
| --- | --- |
| Present simple | I **have** my hair **cut** every month. |
| Present continuous | We**'re having** the house **painted**. |
| Past simple | She **had** her phone **repaired**. |
| Present perfect | I**'ve** just **had** my eyes **tested**. |
| Future | I**'ll get** the documents **translated**. |
| Going to | He**'s going to get** his suit **cleaned**. |

**Get** sedikit lebih informal daripada **have**, maknanya sama.

> **Ingat:** Urutannya **have + OBJECT + V3**, bukan have + V3 + object.

❌ I had cut my hair.
✅ I had my hair cut.

*(I had cut my hair = past perfect: saya sudah memotong rambut sendiri.)*

## B. Pengalaman buruk

Pola yang sama bisa berarti **sesuatu yang tidak diinginkan terjadi** pada subject.

- She **had** her bag **stolen** on the train.
- He **got** his arm **broken** playing football.

## C. Menyuruh orang: have/get/make/let

Jika **orangnya** disebut, polanya berbeda.

:::patterns
have + orang + V1 | I had the mechanic check the brakes. | Menyuruh (biasanya orang yang memang bertugas)
get + orang + to + V1 | I got my brother to help me. | Membujuk / meyakinkan
make + orang + V1 | My mother made me clean my room. | Memaksa
let + orang + V1 | My boss let me leave early. | Mengizinkan
:::

❌ She got him help her.
✅ She got him to help her.

❌ They made me to wait.
✅ They made me wait.

## D. Perbandingan cepat

| Kalimat | Siapa yang mengerjakan? |
| --- | --- |
| I **repaired** my bike. | Saya sendiri |
| My bike **was repaired**. | Seseorang (fokus pada sepeda) |
| I **had** my bike **repaired**. | Orang lain, atas permintaan saya |
| I **had** the mechanic **repair** my bike. | Mekanik, atas permintaan saya |
| I **got** my friend **to repair** my bike. | Teman, setelah saya minta/bujuk |

## E. Kapan memakai causative

Terutama untuk **jasa**: potong rambut, servis kendaraan, membersihkan, memperbaiki, mencetak, menerjemahkan, mengantar.

- I need to **get** my passport **renewed**.
- Where do you **have** your clothes **washed**?
- We **had** pizza **delivered**.

## F. Common Mistakes

### 1. Urutan object dan V3
❌ I'm going to have repaired my laptop.
✅ I'm going to have my laptop repaired.

### 2. Memakai V1/V-ing, bukan V3
❌ She had her nails paint.
✅ She had her nails painted.

### 3. Get + orang tanpa to
❌ I got the waiter bring more water.
✅ I got the waiter to bring more water.

### 4. Make/let + to
❌ My parents let me to go.
✅ My parents let me go.

## Ringkasan

- **have/get + benda + V3** = dikerjakan orang lain untukmu.
- **have + orang + V1**; **get + orang + to V1**.
- **make** (memaksa) dan **let** (mengizinkan) + orang + **V1**.
- Juga untuk kejadian buruk: She had her bag stolen.
