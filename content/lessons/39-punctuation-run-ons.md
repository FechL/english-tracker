# Punctuation & Run-ons

Tanda baca menunjukkan **di mana satu ide berakhir dan ide lain dimulai**. Kesalahan yang paling sering adalah menggabungkan dua kalimat lengkap tanpa penghubung yang benar.

> **Kunci:** Dua klausa lengkap (masing-masing punya S + V) **tidak boleh** digabung hanya dengan koma. Pakai titik, titik koma, atau koma + conjunction.

## A. Tiga kesalahan kalimat

| Jenis | Masalah | Contoh salah |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Run-on (fused sentence) | Dua klausa tanpa tanda baca | I love coffee I drink it every day. |
| Comma splice | Dua klausa hanya dengan koma | I love coffee, I drink it every day. |
| Fragment | Bukan kalimat lengkap | Because I love coffee. |

:::breakdown
I love coffee | klausa 1 (S + V)
I drink it every day | klausa 2 (S + V)
= Dua klausa lengkap. Harus dipisah atau dihubungkan dengan benar.
:::

## B. Empat cara memperbaiki run-on

:::patterns
Titik | I love coffee. I drink it every day. | Dua kalimat terpisah
Koma + FANBOYS | I love coffee, so I drink it every day. | and, but, or, so, yet...
Titik koma | I love coffee; I drink it every day. | Dua ide yang sangat berkaitan
Subordinating conjunction | Because I love coffee, I drink it every day. | Satu klausa menjadi dependent
:::

### Hati-hati dengan however, therefore
**However, therefore, moreover** bukan conjunction. Tidak bisa menghubungkan klausa dengan koma saja.

❌ It was raining, however we went out.
✅ It was raining. However, we went out.
✅ It was raining; however, we went out.

## C. Aturan koma penting

| Aturan | Contoh |
| --- | --- |
| Setelah dependent clause di awal | **When I arrived,** everyone was asleep. |
| Sebelum FANBOYS yang menghubungkan dua klausa | She was tired**,** but she kept working. |
| Daftar tiga item atau lebih | bread, eggs**,** and milk |
| Setelah kata/frasa pembuka | **However,** ... / **In my opinion,** ... |
| Non-defining relative clause | My brother**, who lives in Bali,** is a chef. |
| Sebelum kutipan langsung | She said**,** "I'm ready." |

### Jangan pakai koma
- Antara subject dan verb: ❌ *The man in the red shirt, is my uncle.*
- Sebelum dependent clause di akhir: ❌ *I stayed home, because it rained.* *(umumnya tanpa koma)*

## D. Apostrof

| Fungsi | Contoh |
| --- | --- |
| Kontraksi | don't, it's (= it is), they're (= they are) |
| Kepemilikan | Maya's book, the students' books |
| **Bukan** untuk jamak | ❌ apple's → ✅ apples |

Ingat: **its** (milik) tanpa apostrof; **it's** = it is.

## E. Kapital

- Awal kalimat dan kata **I**.
- Nama orang, tempat, bahasa, kebangsaan: **Indonesian**, **English**, **Japanese**.
- Hari dan bulan: **Monday**, **August**.
- **Bukan** untuk musim: summer, winter.

❌ I speak indonesian and english.
✅ I speak Indonesian and English.

## F. Common Mistakes

### 1. Comma splice
❌ She's very smart, she always gets good grades.
✅ She's very smart, so she always gets good grades.

### 2. Fragment
❌ I went home early. Because I was sick.
✅ I went home early because I was sick.

### 3. However dengan koma saja
❌ I studied hard, however I failed.
✅ I studied hard; however, I failed.

### 4. Apostrof untuk jamak
❌ We sell fresh vegetable's.
✅ We sell fresh vegetables.

## Ringkasan

- Dua klausa lengkap → **titik**, **titik koma**, **koma + FANBOYS**, atau **subordinator**.
- **However/therefore** butuh titik atau titik koma sebelumnya.
- Koma setelah dependent clause di awal, tidak di antara subject dan verb.
- Apostrof untuk kontraksi & kepemilikan, **bukan** jamak.
