# Present Perfect Continuous

Present perfect continuous menekankan **aktivitas yang berlangsung** dari masa lalu sampai sekarang (atau baru saja berhenti) dan **durasinya**.

Kalau present perfect fokus pada **hasil**, present perfect continuous fokus pada **proses**.

> **Kunci:** **have/has been + V-ing**. Pakai untuk durasi aktivitas (How long...?) atau aktivitas yang baru selesai dan bekasnya masih terlihat.

## A. Bentuk

:::breakdown
She | S
has been studying | V
English | O
for three years | A (durasi)
= Dia sudah belajar bahasa Inggris selama tiga tahun (dan masih).
:::

| | I / you / we / they | he / she / it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Positif | I**'ve been waiting**. | She**'s been waiting**. |
| Negatif | I **haven't been waiting**. | She **hasn't been waiting**. |
| Pertanyaan | **Have** you **been waiting**? | **Has** she **been waiting**? |

## B. Kapan dipakai

### 1. Aktivitas yang dimulai dulu dan masih berlangsung
- I**'ve been waiting** for you **for** an hour!
- It**'s been raining since** this morning.
- How long **have** you **been learning** English?

### 2. Aktivitas yang baru berhenti, bekasnya terlihat
- Your eyes are red. **Have** you **been crying**?
- I'm tired because I**'ve been running**.
- The ground is wet. It**'s been raining**.

### 3. Aktivitas berulang belakangan ini
- She**'s been working** late **recently**.
- I**'ve been feeling** tired **lately**.

## C. Perfect continuous vs perfect simple

| Present Perfect Continuous | Present Perfect Simple |
| --- | --- |
| Fokus pada **aktivitas/durasi** | Fokus pada **hasil/jumlah** |
| How **long**...? | How **many/much**...? |
| I**'ve been reading** this book all day. | I**'ve read** 200 pages. |
| She**'s been writing** emails since 9. | She**'s written** 15 emails. |
| Bisa belum selesai | Sudah selesai |

❌ I've been reading three books this month.
✅ I've read three books this month. *(jumlah → simple)*

## D. State verbs: tetap perfect simple

State verbs (know, have [milik], like, believe, own, be) tidak memakai -ing.

❌ I've been knowing her for ten years.
✅ I've known her for ten years.

❌ She's been having that car since 2019.
✅ She's had that car since 2019.

Untuk beberapa verb seperti **live, work, teach, study**, kedua bentuk sama-sama benar dengan for/since:

- I**'ve lived** here for five years. ≈ I**'ve been living** here for five years.

Bentuk continuous terasa sedikit lebih **sementara**.

## E. For, since, how long

:::patterns
have/has been + V-ing + for + durasi | We've been talking for two hours. | Berapa lama
have/has been + V-ing + since + titik awal | He's been working here since May. | Sejak kapan
How long + have/has + S + been + V-ing? | How long have you been waiting? | Pertanyaan durasi
:::

## F. Common Mistakes

### 1. Lupa been
❌ I have waiting for you.
✅ I have been waiting for you.

### 2. Present continuous untuk durasi sampai sekarang
❌ I am waiting here for two hours.
✅ I have been waiting here for two hours.

Ini kesalahan sangat umum dari "saya sudah menunggu selama dua jam".

### 3. Continuous untuk jumlah
❌ She has been drinking four cups of coffee today.
✅ She has drunk four cups of coffee today.

### 4. State verb
❌ How long have you been knowing him?
✅ How long have you known him?

## Ringkasan

- **have/has been + V-ing** = aktivitas + durasi sampai sekarang.
- Juga untuk aktivitas yang baru berhenti dengan **bekas terlihat**.
- **How long** → continuous; **how many/much** → perfect simple.
- State verbs tetap perfect simple.
